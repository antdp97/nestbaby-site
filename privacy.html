NestBaby

Privacy policy

Effective 28 August 2026.

NestBaby is a family baby-tracking app. It stores what you type about your baby so that everyone you invite sees the same record. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data with anyone outside your family.

What the app stores

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email address and, if the provider shares it, your name. These identify you inside your family (“logged by Aygün”).
  • Your baby's profile. Name, sex, date of birth, due date, time zone, and a photo if you add one.
  • Everything you log. Feeds, sleep, nappies, care activities, growth measurements, temperatures, vaccine visits and doses, supplements, notes and any custom activities you create.
  • Your family. Who is in it, their role, and invite codes you send.
  • Reminders. If push reminders are switched on for your family, a device token that lets the server reach your phone.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and on servers run by Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage). Sign-in is handled by Apple or Google. If push reminders are enabled, notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

Who can see it

Only the people in your family — the people you invited, or who invited you. Nobody else, and not us, except as needed to run and support the service.

Sharing you choose

Exports (CSV, PDF) are created on your phone and shared only when and where you choose to share them.

Children

NestBaby is used by parents and carers, not by children. The information about your baby is entered by you, is yours, and is deleted when you delete it.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account → your own name → Delete my account. This starts a 30-day window; sign in again before it ends and nothing is removed. After 30 days your sign-in is deleted. Your baby's record is deleted too if nobody else in the family can still reach it; otherwise it stays with the family, and your entries stay in the record, no longer shown under your name. You can also remove a photo or any individual entry at any time.

Not medical advice

Growth, health and developmental content in the app is informational only. Talk to your paediatrician.
