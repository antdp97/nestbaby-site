Night mode

03:12

Three buttons, amber light, nothing else.

When the baby wakes at night, all you need is Feed, Sleep and Diaper. Amber on black won't dazzle you or wake the person next to you. Log it and go back to sleep.

  • Tap to wake the screenThe resting screen shows only how long the baby has slept.
  • TonightEverything logged since the night began, readable without turning on a light.
  • Up for the dayIn the morning, NestBaby hands back the whole night: how long, the longest stretch, and how many feeds and changes.
