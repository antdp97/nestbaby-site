NestBaby

Delete your account

NestBaby (app io.github.antdp97.nestbaby, developed by Nghia Dang) lets you delete your account and its data at any time. There are two ways to ask.

In the app

  1. Open NestBaby and go to the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to the Account group and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out and the deletion is scheduled.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email dangnghia25197@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my account. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account is permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. Signing in again before the 30 days are up cancels the deletion; nothing is removed until then.

After 30 days the following are deleted permanently and cannot be recovered:

  • Your sign-in (email address, name, and the Google or Apple identity linked to it).
  • Your baby's record — every logged entry, growth measurement, vaccine record and photo — if nobody else in the family can still reach it.
  • Any invite codes you created, and this phone's reminder settings.

What is kept

If another caregiver still has access to the baby's record, that record stays with them — it is their data too. Entries you logged stay in the record but are no longer shown under your name.

You can remove any individual entry or photo yourself at any time, without deleting your account.
