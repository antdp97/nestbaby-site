Common questions

My partner can't see the baby. Open Account → Invite someone and send them the code; codes work once and expire after 7 days.

Entries say “couldn't send”. The server refused that change. Open Home — the callout there leads to a repair screen where each one can be retried or discarded.

I want my data gone. Account → your own name → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what happens next.
